This page reads your phone's compass and tilt sensors to figure out which star you're pointing at — like a planetarium app in your pocket. No camera, no internet beyond this page.

Your phone will ask permission to share orientation and location. Best used at night, outdoors. Keep moving away from large metal objects (cars, lampposts) for best compass accuracy.
